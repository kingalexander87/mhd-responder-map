How the forecast works. Demand = (open Rebook + ECNS + New Business deals) × stage probability × workload (testing_days_quantity × of_techs), bucketed into the quarter of the deal's expected start date. Stage probabilities are HubSpot-style: Qualification 10%, Proposal Sent 30%, Scheduling 70%, Scheduled 95%. Capacity = schedulable responders per region × 60 working days/quarter. Gap = demand − capacity; positive means the region needs more responders. Hiring suggestion assumes each new responder adds 60 tech-days of quarterly capacity.
